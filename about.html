About Solaris Energy Group

Industrial solar expertise from Stuttgart. 15 years of delivering reliable energy solutions across Europe and beyond.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To accelerate the adoption of high-performance industrial solar systems by delivering reliable JA Solar technology, complete factory installations, and trusted electrical solutions that help manufacturers reduce energy costs and environmental impact.

Our Vision

A future where industrial facilities across Europe and selected global markets operate on clean, affordable, and resilient solar power — supported by long-term partnerships and technical excellence.
